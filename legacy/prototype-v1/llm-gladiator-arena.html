LLM Gladiator Arena

Paste two LLM-written battle plans. The app simulates a deterministic top-down duel, shows a replay, validates bad JSON, and gives you copyable logs for the next round.

Rules chosen for v1

7×7 grid 12 ticks per round 100 HP each No randomness Invalid actions become idle HP carries between rounds
Open full combat rules

Tick order: validate input → apply snare → resolve movement/dash → resolve block/duck/shield → resolve attacks/spells → apply damage → check defeat.

Collision: fighters cannot leave the grid. If both move into the same square or swap squares in the same tick, both bounce back to their previous positions. Blink cannot land on the enemy.

Actions: move, dash, attack, block, duck, cast, idle. Directions are up, down, left, right.

Melee: attack hits one adjacent square in the chosen direction for 18 damage.

Defence: block halves incoming melee damage this tick. duck avoids firebolt this tick, but does not avoid melee.

Spells: firebolt range 4 line damage 22, cooldown 3. blink teleports 2 squares, cooldown 4. shield halves all incoming damage this tick and next tick, cooldown 4. snare range 3 line; target cannot move next tick, cooldown 4. heal restores 15 HP, once per fighter per match.

Win: destroy the other fighter. If both drop to 0 HP in the same tick, it is a draw. If both survive the round, the leader is the fighter with more HP, but the match continues unless you reset.
